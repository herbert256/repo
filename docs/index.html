How to use this book

No plant in this book has ever grown. Each is raised from its plate number alone — the same plant for every reader, on every day — and engraved afresh, line by line, whenever its page is opened.

  • ← → turn the pages  ·  R open at random
  • S hear the plant’s song — it keeps singing as you turn the pages
  • Gather pollen on one plate, turn to another and pollinate it to raise a hybrid. Hybrids can be crossed again.
  • Click a plate while it is being engraved to finish it at once.
